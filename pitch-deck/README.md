# Pitch deck: Why hire Rashmi Singh

A nine-slide personal pitch for hiring managers. It answers one question, "Why should an employer hire me?", in four to six minutes.

- **Live deck:** <https://claude.ai/artifact/8K1rrGYFuhMQqYfKH5nUrN> (private until shared from its Share menu; it can be downloaded there as PowerPoint or PDF)
- **Source:** the files in `project/` in this folder

## The message

| | |
|---|---|
| Central idea | I bring the business understanding and delivery mindset that turn technology into business outcomes |
| Reason 1 | I understand the business |
| Reason 2 | I turn plans into execution |
| Reason 3 | I am building for what comes next |
| The one thing to remember | A translator between the business and the build |
| The formula | Business understanding + Project delivery + Technology curiosity = Technology that gets used |

The move into technology is presented as an expansion of existing capability, not a restart. The deck never presents Rashmi as a technical specialist.

The deck does not claim any time saving from the prototype, because none has been measured.

## Design

Same identity as the portfolio website: white ground, green-black text, emerald accent and a mint highlight, with one typeface (Instrument Sans). Dark slides are used three times only: the hook, the proof and the close. The mint box on slide 6 marks the human decision, which is the point of the case study.

## Editing the deck

- **In the browser:** open the live deck and edit text directly on a slide.
- **In the files:** each slide is one file in `project/slides/`. Shared styles are in `project/deck.css`, and the slide order is in `project/deck.json`.

Before presenting, replace anything you cannot speak to comfortably. If a figure changes on the CV, change it here too.

## Before you use it

- Rehearse aloud with a timer. The notes are a guide, not a script.
- Tailor slide 8 to the role: name the team's actual priorities if you know them.
- Share the deck from its Share menu, or download it, before sending a link to anyone. The link is private by default.
