The first 90 days

How I would start.

Days 1 – 30

Understand

Learn the business, the team, the processes and the priorities.

Meet the stakeholders and map who needs what.

Days 31 – 60

Contribute

Take on coordination of running work.

Bring forward one or two improvement opportunities, with the evidence.

Days 61 – 90

Deliver

Help carry one defined initiative through execution.

Report progress, risks and open decisions clearly.

A proposed approach, to be shaped with you. It is not a promise of specific results.

Rashmi Singh
