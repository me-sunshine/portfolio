When I planned the Operations Assistant case study, the budget was zero. That turned tool selection into a licensing question as much as a technical one.

- **n8n** is free to self-host, but its Sustainable Use License is not an open-source licence.
- **Activepieces** is MIT-licensed at its core, with enterprise features under a separate commercial licence.
- **Ollama** is MIT-licensed, and the model I used, Qwen2.5 7B, is Apache 2.0.

"Free" and "open source" are different claims, and a project manager should know which one a business is relying on.
