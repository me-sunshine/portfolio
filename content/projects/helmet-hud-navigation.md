---
title: Motorbike Helmet with Heads-Up Display Navigation and Fall Detection
summary: A smart helmet concept that shows navigation inside the rider's view and raises an alert with the rider's location after a fall. Published as a peer-reviewed conference paper by Springer.
period: 2019 – 2020
role: Co-author
problem: Two-wheeler use is rising worldwide, for transport and for recreation. A conventional helmet only cushions an impact. It does nothing to stop riders looking away from the road to check directions, and nothing to bring help quickly after a crash.
contribution: Co-authored the paper with three others (Ritik Gupta, Vaibhav E. Pawar and Shital V. Patel) during my mechanical engineering degree.
technologies: [Heads-up display, GPS, Google Maps, Fall detection, Internet of Things]
outcomes:
  - Proposed a helmet with a heads-up display for distraction-free navigation.
  - Proposed a fall detection system that shares accurate location information within minutes of an incident, to allow a faster emergency response.
  - Published in the proceedings of the Second International Conference on Computer Networks and Communication Technologies (ICCNCT 2019), Lecture Notes on Data Engineering and Communications Technologies, Springer, 2020, pages 185–193.
