title: Mumbai Ploggers
summary: A citizen-driven movement I founded in 2020 that combines jogging with litter pick-up across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai.
period: 2020 – present
role: Founder
problem: Mumbai's public spaces carry a heavy litter load, and awareness campaigns alone rarely change behaviour. People needed a simple, repeatable way to act.
contribution: I started the movement at 23, planned and ran the clean-up drives, built the weekly operating process, and created a chapter model so volunteers could lead drives in their own areas.
technologies: [Volunteer coordination, Event logistics, Partnership management, Social media]
outcomes:
  - 500+ volunteers engaged and 100+ clean-up drives planned.
  - Self-sustained for more than four years without external funding.
  - Partnerships with 15+ corporates, 10+ educational institutions and several NGOs, delivering 50+ awareness sessions and workshops.
  - UN Volunteers Award (2021) and Planet Buddy recognition at the Economic Times SDGs Summit (2021).

What began as a personal habit became an operation with weekly drives, corporate and college partners, and volunteer leaders running their own chapters. The lesson I took into every later role: a process that other people can run without you is worth more than one you run well yourself.
